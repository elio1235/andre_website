Our Story

About André

A corner café where the coals never quite go cold and nobody's in a hurry.

It started in 1998 with one brass pipe, a handful of regulars, and André's stubborn belief that a good chicha is built slowly. No shortcuts, no rushing the bowl.

Today the room is fuller and the menu is longer, but the ritual is the same. We pack every bowl by hand, swap the coals at the right moment, and let the evening take its time. You're not a customer here — you're a guest.
